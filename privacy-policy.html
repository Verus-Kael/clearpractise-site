Privacy Policy

Plain version: this website collects nothing about you by default. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing switched on.

What this site is

A static site served via GitHub Pages. When you load a page, the web server that serves it records standard transient logs (IP address and requested file) as an automatic part of serving any web page; we do not analyse, profile from, or sell that data. Email links on this site go to our own mailbox.

If you email us

  • We keep your message and address so we can reply to you.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your details with third parties for marketing.
  • We delete enquiry correspondence on request, and you can ask what we hold at any time.

Links to other sites

Some pages contain links to third parties (for example, our hosting and software partners). Some of these are affiliate links: if you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend services we use ourselves, and their privacy practices are theirs.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete any personal data of yours we hold, at any time, by emailing enquiries@clearpractise.com. We respond personally and promptly.

Changes

If this policy ever changes in a way that matters, the change will be stated here in plain language, not buried.
