About CPWS

We build websites and we host them. We run full IT and web services for businesses that want their technology handled properly.

Our main offer: websites and hosting

We design and build websites, and we host them on hardened, minimal-dependency infrastructure. If you already have a site somewhere else, we migrate it for you at no charge as part of onboarding. You get a site that loads fast, carries nothing it doesn't need, and lives on servers we look after like they were ours - because they are.

Everything else we do

  • Security audits - a hard look at your current setup: what you run, what it exposes, what's out of date, what an attacker would try first. Written findings, plain English, prioritised fixes.
  • Server builds - custom hardened servers for larger companies: designed, built, documented, and handed over or managed by us. The same stack philosophy we run our own platform on.
  • Full IT and web services - ongoing work across the breadth of business technology: domains, email, migrations, integrations, maintenance. If it connects a business to the internet, we deal with it.

Who we do it for

Anyone with a website. Small businesses that need a proper home for their first site. Established companies whose hosting hasn't been thought about in years. Larger organisations that need a server built to a standard their internal team doesn't have time to research. Practices and professionals who handle sensitive client data and can't afford a breach.

How we work

Lean, hardened infrastructure. Isolated systems. Data hosted in the EEA. No third-party analytics on the sites we build, and no data mining, ever. Direct access to the person doing the work - no ticket queues, no junior hand-offs.

Honesty first

Nothing online is bulletproof. But every script, tracker and plug-in you don't load is one less thing that can hurt you. We won't promise you the impossible; we'll build you the smallest, hardest target we can, and tell you honestly what's left.
