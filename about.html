About Clear Practise

Clear Practise exists for one reason: the people who do the most sensitive work in society — therapists, counselors, journalists, whistleblowers, wellness practitioners — are being served by an internet built to exploit them.

Your clients trust you with their deepest struggles. Then that trust flows into a website running on shared hosting, feeding tracking pixels, analytics harvesters, and data brokers. That is not a privacy policy problem. It is an architecture problem.

What We Do

We build and host privacy-first websites for practices that cannot afford leaks. Sovereign infrastructure in the EEA. Zero third-party tracking. GDPR compliance by design, not by disclaimer. Every client site runs in its own isolated, encrypted container — no shared databases, no noisy neighbours.

Why Anonymous?

"We judge ourselves by our work, not our face."

Clear Practise's founder chooses to remain anonymous while the business is being built. Not to hide from accountability — the opposite. In a market where competitors harvest their users' data, anonymity is a statement of what we don't collect, don't sell, and don't need. Our clients interact with the work itself: the infrastructure, the security, the support. When you contact Clear Practise, you reach a human being directly. No ticket queues. No call centres.

Our Promise

Your life can change without your privacy being the price.

Everything we build — hosting, web design, compliance — exists to honour that sentence. No exceptions.
