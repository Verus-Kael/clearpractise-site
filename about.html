About CPWS

CPWS builds highly secure, minimal-dependency web stacks for businesses that can't afford to be the next headline.

Why we exist

This year, major brands were breached through the third-party scripts they chose to embed. Most web agencies sell convenience and bolt on security afterwards. We start from the opposite end: fewer dependencies, fewer doors, no strangers in your stack.

How we work

Lean, hardened infrastructure. Isolated instances. Data hosted in the EEA. No third-party analytics on the sites we build, and no data mining, ever. We are judged by our work, not our marketing.

Honesty first

Nothing online is unhackable. Attack surface, however, is a design choice. We won't promise you the impossible; we'll build you the smallest, hardest target we can.
