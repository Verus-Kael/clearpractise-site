How We Isolate Every Client Instance (Without Shared Resources)

April 6, 2026 • 8 min read

Most "secure" hosting providers claim privacy while secretly sharing databases, CPU cycles, and memory pools between clients. This is a fundamental flaw for mental health professionals who need absolute data sovereignty.

At Clear Practise, we engineered a better way: Sovereign Container Isolation.

The Problem with Shared Hosting

In a typical shared hosting environment:

  • Shared Database: Your client data sits in the same PostgreSQL instance as dozens of other therapists. A misconfigured query by one client could expose another's data.
  • Shared Memory: If one client's application leaks memory, other sites slow down or crash.
  • Shared Filesystem: A vulnerability in one client's software could allow an attacker to read another client's files.
The Reality: Even "managed" WordPress hosts often share underlying infrastructure. True isolation requires dedicated resources.

Our Solution: Per-Client Containers

When you sign up for Clear Practise, you get a dedicated, isolated container with its own:

  • Private Database Instance: A dedicated database container. No shared schemas. No cross-tenant queries possible.
  • Isolated Filesystem: Your files live in a container with no access to other clients' data.
  • Network Air-Gap: Client A cannot ping, connect to, or even see Client B's container. Hard network segmentation enforced by default.
  • Dedicated CPU/Memory Quotas: Your resources are reserved for you. No "noisy neighbor" effects.

Why This Matters for Therapists

As a mental health professional, you are bound by strict confidentiality laws (GDPR, HIPAA). A data breach isn't just an IT issue—it's a legal and ethical catastrophe.

With Clear Practise:

  • Zero Risk of Cross-Tenant Leakage: Your data is physically separated from others.
  • Audit Trails: Every access to your container is logged and immutable.
  • Compliance Ready: Our architecture is designed to meet the strictest regulatory requirements.
